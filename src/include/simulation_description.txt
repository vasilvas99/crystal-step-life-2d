This simulation provides a dynamic environment for MC+CA crystal growth models. The simulation includes
the following two events:
    - Nucleation Event (attachment to one, A21) : This event simulates the attachment of a particle to a crystal surface
    where the particle requires a single neighboring particle in the crystal lattice to attach.
    - Crystallization Event (attachment to kink, A2K): This event simulates the attachment of a particle to a crystal surface
    to kink sites.
The simulation parameters include:
    - c0 - Initial concentration of particles in the solution (maintained constant throughout the simulation)
    - Pa - Probability of attachment to one neighbor (nucleation event)
    - Pk - Probability of attachment to kink site (crystallization event)
    - nds - Number of diffusional steps per simulation step
The Pa probability can be modulated in two ways:
    - Constant Pa: The attachment probability remains constant along the crystal surface (lattice row)
    - Periodic Pa: The attachment probability varies periodically along the crystal surface (lattice row) with sin^2 modulation.
    At this point an additional control parameter is introduced - the wavenumber of the modulation. This is visualized
    as an (inverted) sine wave at the top of the simulation grid. The peaks of the wave correspond to regions of high
    A21 probability.
